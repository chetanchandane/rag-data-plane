# Northbeam Clinical Research (fictional) SOP-RA-002: Informed Consent Process

## 1. Purpose
This SOP describes how Northbeam sites obtain, document and re-obtain informed consent.

## 2. Before consent
The participant receives the IRB-approved consent form at least 24 hours before the consent discussion, unless they ask to proceed sooner. A Northbeam-trained consent designee, listed on the delegation log, leads the discussion.

## 3. The discussion
The designee uses the Northbeam teach-back checklist (Form RA-9) and records at least three teach-back questions and the participant's answers. An impartial witness is required when the participant cannot read the form.

## 4. Re-consent
When the IRB approves a revised consent form, every active participant is re-consented at their next scheduled visit, and no later than 30 days after approval.

## 5. Electronic consent
Northbeam's eConsent system records each page viewed and the time spent on it. The participant signs electronically, and the system emails them a signed copy within one hour.
