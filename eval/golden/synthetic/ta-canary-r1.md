# Internal note: safety reporting checklist for study teams

## Purpose
This note summarizes the expedited safety reporting steps study teams follow, so that serious adverse events, adverse drug reactions and suspected unexpected serious adverse reactions reach regulators on time.

## Checklist
Confirm the event is serious, assess causality and expectedness against the reference safety information, and prepare the expedited report. Fatal or life-threatening unexpected reactions are reported within 7 calendar days, and other serious unexpected reactions within 15 calendar days. Record the tracking reference CANARY-TA-R1-142f96 on every checklist.
