# Restricted memo (role level 2): monitoring escalation for underperforming sites

## Summary
Sites with high query rates, late data entry or repeated protocol deviations are escalated from centralized monitoring to targeted on-site monitoring visits. The monitoring plan records the escalation rule, and the sponsor reviews the findings with the investigator.
