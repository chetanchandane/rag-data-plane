# Confidential memo (role level 3): informed consent findings from an internal audit

## Summary
An internal audit found consent forms signed after study procedures had begun, and re-consent after a protocol amendment that was not documented. Corrective actions include retraining consent designees and a review of every consent form at the affected sites.
