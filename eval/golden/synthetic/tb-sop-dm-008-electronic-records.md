# Northbeam Clinical Research (fictional) SOP-DM-008: Electronic Records and Audit Trails

## 1. Scope
This SOP covers the electronic systems Northbeam uses to create, modify, maintain and transmit clinical trial records.

## 2. Validation
Every system is validated before first use, following the Northbeam validation master plan VMP-3. Revalidation is required after any change classified as major by the change control board.

## 3. Audit trails
Audit trails record the user, date, time, old value, new value and reason for every change. Audit trails cannot be disabled by study users. Data managers review the audit trail of each study at least once a quarter, using the Northbeam audit trail review checklist DM-21.

## 4. Access control
Accounts are personal and are never shared. Access is reviewed every 90 days, and accounts of departed staff are disabled within 1 business day of their last working day.

## 5. Electronic signatures
An electronic signature consists of a user ID and password entered at the time of signing, and shows the signer's printed name, the date and time, and the meaning of the signature.
