# Northbeam Clinical Research (fictional) SOP-QA-005: Protocol Deviation Management

## 1. Definitions
A protocol deviation is any change, divergence or departure from the approved study design or procedures. Northbeam classifies deviations as important or not important; an important deviation may significantly affect the completeness, accuracy or reliability of key study data, or a participant's rights, safety or well-being.

## 2. Recording
Site staff log every deviation in the Northbeam deviation tracker within 5 business days of discovery. Important deviations are also reported to the sponsor within 2 business days.

## 3. Review
The Northbeam quality assurance group reviews the deviation tracker monthly. Three or more important deviations of the same type at one site trigger a corrective and preventive action (CAPA) plan, due within 30 days.

## 4. Training
Staff at a site with an open CAPA plan complete targeted retraining before enrolling further participants.
