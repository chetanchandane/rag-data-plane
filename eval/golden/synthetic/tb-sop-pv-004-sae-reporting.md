# Northbeam Clinical Research (fictional) SOP-PV-004: Serious Adverse Event Reporting

## 1. Purpose
This standard operating procedure describes how Northbeam site staff identify, record and report serious adverse events (SAEs) in the studies Northbeam manages for its sponsors. Northbeam is a fictional contract research organization created for testing; none of its procedures are real.

## 2. Internal timelines
Site staff must enter every SAE into the Northbeam safety portal within 12 hours of first awareness, using Form PV-17. The Northbeam safety desk reviews each Form PV-17 within 4 business hours and forwards it to the sponsor's pharmacovigilance contact. Follow-up information is due every 5 calendar days until the event is resolved or stabilized.

## 3. Causality and expectedness
The site investigator records a causality assessment on a four-point scale: not related, unlikely, possible, probable. Expectedness is judged against the current investigator's brochure. Northbeam's safety physician may upgrade, but never downgrade, the investigator's causality assessment.

## 4. Escalation
If the safety portal is unavailable, staff telephone the Northbeam safety desk at extension 4417 and file Form PV-17 on paper, scanning it within 24 hours. Any SAE involving a death is escalated to the Northbeam medical director immediately, in addition to the normal route.

## 5. Records
Northbeam retains SAE source records for 15 years after study close-out, in the Halvard document archive.
