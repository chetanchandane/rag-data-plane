# Northbeam Clinical Research (fictional) SOP-CM-011: Risk-Based Monitoring Plan

## 1. Scope
This SOP sets out how Northbeam builds the monitoring plan for each study, combining centralized monitoring with targeted on-site visits.

## 2. Key risk indicators
Northbeam tracks six key risk indicators per site: query rate, adverse event reporting rate, protocol deviation rate, screen failure rate, data entry lag and missed visits. A site is flagged amber when any indicator crosses its threshold for two consecutive months, and red when three indicators are amber at once. The data entry lag threshold is 7 calendar days.

## 3. Visit frequency
Sites flagged red receive an on-site visit within 21 days. Green sites receive a remote review every 6 weeks and an on-site visit at least once every 6 months. The first patient visit at every site is always monitored on site.

## 4. Source data verification
Northbeam verifies 100 percent of informed consent forms and primary endpoint data, and a random 20 percent sample of all other data points, rising to 50 percent at red sites.

## 5. Monitoring reports
Monitors file the visit report in the Northbeam CTMS within 10 business days of each visit; the clinical lead signs it within 5 further business days.
