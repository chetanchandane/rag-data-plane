# Site training notes: electronic informed consent workflow

## Overview
Electronic informed consent (eConsent) lets participants review the consent material on a tablet or their own device, with interactive elements such as videos and knowledge checks. The investigator remains responsible for confirming that the participant understood the information before they sign.

## Notes for site staff
Verify the participant's identity before the eConsent session, keep a record of the version of the consent material the participant viewed, and give the participant a copy of the signed consent.
